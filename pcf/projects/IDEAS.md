# Ideas: hypotheses to test

> The discovery loop's inbox (CLAUDE.md, "Two loops"). The CEO and Claude both add lines. Claude checks them cheaply, in batches at each retro (CLAUDE.md, "The retro"), and records the answer here.

**How this differs from the registry and the roadmap:**

| | Registry and roadmap | Ideas |
|---|---|---|
| Holds | Projects: work with a brief, code, results and dependencies | Hypotheses: claims about football or our models that might be true |
| Size | Days to weeks | Minutes to an hour to check |
| Question | What are we building, and when? | What might be true, and how would we know? |
| Ends | Done, with an interface card | Killed (with a one-line result), folded into a project's brief, or grown into a project of its own |

- **An idea that survives a cheap check graduates:** into an existing project's brief, or into the registry as a new project.
- **A killed idea stays here with its result,** so nobody checks it twice.
- **A cheap check is discovery, not proof.** It never counts as a model beating anything; only the harness does that.

Each line: the claim, where it came from (a CEO hunch, a project's misses, a disagreement with ECR or the market, a retro), what result would say it's real, and **what its author expects before the test** (the CEO, 2026-11-15): a confidence (low, medium or high: roughly 60%, 75% or 90% sure) and an expected size in the target's units. For ideas older than 2026-11-15 the expectation is Claude's from retro 1, written after the idea but before its test; ideas already settled show a dash. The patterns cited as P1 to P8 are in [RETRO.md](RETRO.md), which also scores these expectations. The CEO may add a confidence of their own to any line, and an idea the CEO edits significantly becomes a hunch (HUNCHES.md).
