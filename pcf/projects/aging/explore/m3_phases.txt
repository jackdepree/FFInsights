# Method 3: career phases as hidden states (2026-11-25)

Survivor pairs at QB, RB, WR and TE with efficiency in both seasons: 7,301 (1,928 players). Each is a change in pulled usage and pulled
efficiency, standardized by position. A five-state Gaussian hidden Markov model with transitions by age band (under 26, 26-29, 30+), started at rising, steady,
usage-only decline, full decline and efficiency rebound, then learned by EM; one model for the four positions, refit every three target seasons.

## H117, the "told apart in advance" half

Usage decliners (as in method 1) with an efficiency change in both seasons: 716. Kept efficiency: 47%. A logistic on what is known before the
decline season (age, usage and efficiency levels, last season's changes, star status, mileage; then the model's state chances added), walk-forward by target season.

- without the states: held-out AUC 0.847 (90% 0.821 to 0.872), 626 decline seasons.
- with the states: held-out AUC 0.836 (90% 0.812 to 0.864), 626 decline seasons.

## H119: late-career comebacks

After a full-decline season (pulled usage and efficiency both fell from s-1 to s, at or past the position's usage peak), is next season's efficiency above what method 1's
age model already expects (its own pull-back included)? The leftover is next season's centred efficiency less that forecast, weighted by next season's opportunities.

- after a full decline: 765 seasons, mean leftover +0.0110 EPA an opportunity (90% -0.0001 to +0.0211); share above 0: 47%
- every other season: 4326 seasons, mean leftover -0.0051 EPA an opportunity (90% -0.0111 to +0.0004); share above 0: 44%
- Forecastable? the rebound state's chance against the leftover, after full declines (held out, 765): correlation -0.000 (90% -0.051 to +0.050)
- Forecastable? the twin's efficiency forecast against the leftover, after full declines (held out, 765): correlation +0.000 (90% -0.057 to +0.064)
- Forecastable? the analogs' efficiency forecast against the leftover, after full declines (held out, 765): correlation +0.008 (90% -0.049 to +0.060)

## Checks: is it the pull-back?

H117's label (efficiency change at or above 0) and H119's leftover both carry the pull-back to the mean: a season that was high falls next season, and one that was low
rises, from noise alone. Three checks.

- H117 with his pulled efficiency this season alone: held-out AUC 0.810.
- H117 with the label beyond expectation (next season's efficiency at or above the age model's forecast; kept: 39%): held-out AUC 0.536 (90% 0.499 to 0.576), 626 decline seasons, every input and the state chances.
- H119 against a forecast that knows his last two seasons, after a full decline: 765 seasons, mean leftover -0.0019 (90% -0.0132 to +0.0088)
- H119 against a forecast that knows his last two seasons, every other season: 4326 seasons, mean leftover -0.0041 (90% -0.0093 to +0.0011)
