# 0036: context-live, one model per outcome on the situation and the live call fields, with its own play-level runner

**Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Decided by:** the CEO agreed the brief (2026-11-16: every play, runs included; interceptions, fumbles and scrambles left out; screens and play action count as calls; each outcome must beat the situation-only model; schemes-v2's traits allowed). The calls below are Claude's technical calls, listed for veto at merge. · **Project:** `context-live`

**Decision:** `pcf.experts.context_live` gives, for every scrimmage play from 2022 on, an expected value for each of 17 outcomes from the situation and both sides' live call fields, with nothing known about who the players or teams are. Each outcome has its own model, chosen by the ladder and the brief's no-noise test. Each player's and team's average context per game comes with it.

**The calls:**

1. **Its own runner, not the forecast harness** (`run.py`), like the participation maps (decisions 0030, 0031). The harness forecasts games before kickoff; this expert describes plays after they happened. Adding a post-game moment kind to the shared harness is a large change to a module every model depends on (sign-off), and the parallel `crude-forecaster` build is using it. The runner reuses the harness's pieces: the paired comparison by whole weeks (`validation.compare`), the vault rule (`backtest.check_seasons`), calibration and range coverage (`validation.calibration`), consistency, and the recency half-life. Now that three post-game experts exist, a shared post-game mode is logged as an idea (I62).
2. **Point in time.**
   - A play's `as_of` is the first weekly run at least 14 days after kickoff, FTN's known-at (participation-late's `moments`, reused).
   - Inputs come from an in-season view (`play.pbp`, `play.ftn`, `team_season.charted`). Pressure and time to throw are outcomes only, read from a retrospective view of `play.participation` for training and scoring, never as inputs.
   - A training row must be known (inputs and outcome) before the test season's first `as_of`; the runner refuses otherwise.
   - `check_point_in_time` rebuilds sample games from a view at each game's own `as_of` (largest gap in any field must be 0) and checks that a view at the game's morning after holds none of its FTN rows (must be 0).
3. **Refits once per test season**, on every earlier season of the outcome (recency-weighted, half-life 4 seasons, VALIDATION.md §1). The exploration did the same. Weekly refits would add part of the season to 2023's thin training set (2022 alone); not tried, listed in the case against.
4. **Seasons:** plays from 2022 (FTN's first season); scored 2023 and 2024; the vault is 2025. All season types (playoffs included), every scrimmage play with a down, no two-point tries.
5. **Pressure and time to throw start in 2023** (new, not in the exploration). The participation data's provider change (EXPLORATION F2) also changed them: pressure is charted on 29% of 2022's dropbacks and 15% after; time to throw averages 2.79 s and 2.65 s. The exploration trained 2023's pressure on 2022. Here those two are fit on 2023 and scored on 2024 (the vault: fit on 2023-2024).
6. **The outcomes and their populations** (`outcomes.py`): designed runs (a rush that wasn't a dropback): rushing yards, stuffed (0 or fewer), explosive (10+), success, first down, touchdown. Dropbacks: pressure, sack, time to throw, yards, explosive (20+), success, first down, touchdown. Pass attempts (no sacks or scrambles): depth of target, completion. Every snap with a down: penalty. Left out (the CEO's call 2): interceptions, fumbles, scrambles, a dropback's EPA.
7. **The fields** (`plays.py`):
   - **The situation:** down, distance, field position, goal to go, quarter, both clocks, score, both teams' timeouts, home.
   - **The live call fields:** shotgun, no huddle (play-by-play); where the QB lines up, backs in the backfield, motion, play action, screen, RPO, trick play, sneak, hash (FTN, the offense); box count, blitzers, pass rushers (FTN, the defense). FTN's "0" for an uncharted location or hash is missing.
   - **Penalties use the situation only** (EXPLORATION F2: the call fields are charted differently on plays wiped out by a penalty).
   - **Schemes-v2's traits** (the CEO's call 5): last season's `team_season.charted` traits over expected, the offense's for the offense (personnel rates, pistol, time to throw) and the defense's for the defense (box, man, two-high, blitz, rushers, nickel, dime). The over-expected columns, because the raw rates move with the charting by season (schemes-v2#R13).
8. **The rungs** (`run.RUNGS`): `0-base` (the training rate, or mean and quantiles); `1-situation-linear`, `2-situation-trees`; `3-live-linear`, `4-live-trees`; `5-traits-trees`. For H65, `offense-trees` and `defense-trees` (the situation plus one side's calls).
   - **Linear:** logistic or ridge regression; splines (6 knots) for distance, field position, both clocks and the score; one column per value for downs, counts, flags and categories (missing is its own value; values under 30 rows pooled); traits imputed and scaled. Ranges: the mean plus the training residuals' quantiles.
   - **Trees:** histogram gradient boosting with the exploration's settings (150 rounds, learning rate 0.06, 15 leaves, 80 rows a leaf, L2 1.0). Probabilities with log loss; amounts with squared error for the mean and three quantile-loss fits for q10, q50 and q90, sorted so they never cross.
9. **Scores:** log loss for chances, the mean pinball of q10, q50 and q90 for amounts (VALIDATION.md §3). The readable score is the exploration's: the share of the base rate's squared error removed.
10. **What ships per outcome** (`run.choose`, rule k):
    - The situation-only model is the linear rung unless the trees beat it; the live model likewise.
    - The live model ships if it beats the situation-only model (the whole 90% interval of the paired difference, resampled by week, below 0) **and** its gain is larger than the fitting noise (the spread of the gain over 5 refits on training games resampled with replacement).
    - Otherwise the situation-only model ships if it beats the base rate, else the base rate.
    - The traits rung replaces the live model only if it beats it the same way.
11. **Two nulls, 20 refits each, the shipped rung must beat all 20** (VALIDATION.md §5's cheap version): outcomes shuffled within each week (the signal test), and the brief's "shuffled copy of its call fields": every call field of a play moved together to another play of the same week, in training and test, so the calls keep their mix but say nothing about the play.
12. **The output:** `outputs/context-live/<run_id>/history.parquet`, the standard schema keyed by `game_id, play_id`, one row per play and outcome, `quantity` the outcome's name (`pressure_prob`, `rush_yards_exp`, …), `model_version` the shipped rung. A chance's q10-q90 equal it.
13. **Player and team context** (`context.py`): a play's context is the walk-forward expected success (run or dropback) minus the league's rate on the training seasons. A player's is the chance-weighted average over the plays participation-late's map puts him on (its walk-forward history, known 14 days after the game like FTN). participation-late has no MLflow version, so `registry.yaml` pins its history run (`history_runs`, decision 0033's mechanism) and `depends_on` adds it.
14. **The map's personnel is left out** (the brief's step 5). participation-late's expected count of each position group on a play looked like it added 0.3% to 1.7% of the loss on 2024 (fit on 2023). It is a leak: the map pins every player the play-by-play names on the play (the target, the tackler, the sacker, a pass defender) and balances each play to 11, so its chances carry part of the play's result. A rough undoing of the pins made the "gain" larger, not smaller (sack 1.1% to 15.3% of squared error), which confirms the chances carry who was named (`explore/personnel_pins.py`). `run.personnel_check` stays to test a map edition without the play's own names (idea I63).
15. **The vault run scores the rungs chosen on 2023-2024** (`--fixed-from backtest`); nothing is re-chosen on 2025. The rule's own choice on the vault is reported beside it.
16. **The champion** is each outcome's shipped rung refitted on every season through 2025, logged as one model (`models.ContextModel`) and registered only if every check passed (`run.final`). Its forecasts for 2026's plays so far are the live output (`live.parquet`). The outputs aren't in the prediction ledger: they're made after the game, not before kickoff.
17. **Sack and explosive pass are recalibrated** (VALIDATION.md §7). Their calibration slope on 2023-2024 was 0.81 (the bar is 0.9 to 1.1): too sure in both directions. For those two outcomes every rung but the base is fit on the earlier four fifths of its training window (ordered by season and week), Platt scaling (a and b in sigmoid(a + b·logit p)) is fit on the latest fifth, and the rung is refit on the whole window with that scaling on top. In a check before the rerun it took the slopes to 1.00 and 0.95 with log loss unchanged (sack +0.0002, explosive pass −0.0001). It is not applied everywhere: on pressure it made the error 4.8 points (2023 alone trains 2024, and its last fifth isn't like the rest) and on completion it pushed the slope to 1.15. The two outcomes were picked on 2023-2024's calibration, so the vault (2025) is the check that it holds.
18. **Yards forecasts stay on the field.** Rushing and dropback yards are clipped to the distance to the end zone, a target's depth to the back of the end zone (10 more), and all three to no more lost than the distance to the offense's own goal line. The first backtest had 22 runs, 79 dropbacks and 220 targets whose mean went past those, and 644, 779 and 1,315 whose q90 did (rule h). The clip applies to every rung, the base included: a base rate that forecasts 4.2 yards from the 2-yard line is not a football forecast. That makes the base smarter, so the shares against it are smaller than the exploration's: rushing yards 2.5% to 1.2% for the live model and 2.3% to 1.0% for the situation, dropback yards 2.2% to 1.6%. About half of what "the situation explains" in a run's yards was the end of the field. The comparisons that decide what ships (live against the situation) hardly move (rushing yards: 0.68% to 0.69% of the loss).

**Alternatives:**
- **Through the shared harness with a post-game moment.** Cleaner long-term; a large shared change during a parallel build (call 1).
- **One multi-output model** (all outcomes from one tree ensemble). Outcomes have different populations and kinds; one model per outcome is the brief's method A.
- **Recalibrating every chance, or isotonic regression instead of Platt.** Every chance: worse on pressure and completion (call 17). Isotonic: more flexible, needs more rows than a fifth of 2022's sacks.
- **Leaving the trees' over-shoot at the top of rushing yards** as a reported miss rather than a fix: the top tenth of the trees' expected rushing yards runs about a yard and a third above what happened (the linear rung doesn't); the range score, which is what's scored, passes. Chosen: reported, not fixed (RESULTS.md).
- **Pressure and time to throw trained on 2022 too, with a season flag.** Two seasons of the new provider is little, but a flag can't fix a definition that halved; mixing would teach the model the wrong base rate.
